# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS: YUNIX STORE MVP

## 1. VISION
To be the dominant, all-in-one premium POS for Kenyan restaurants by augmenting the legacy uniCenta POS with a modern, powerful sidecar Store Controller.

## 2. THE 5 MAJOR HEADACHES (MVP FOCUS)
1. **Blind Store (Requisitions)**: Digital handover between Store (Desktop) and Kitchen (11-inch Tablet). Requisitions must explicitly tag if the items are intended for "Sales" or "Staff Meals" to separate costs.
2. **The Debt Trap (Supplier Ledger)**: Replacing manual Excel sheets. The system must manage a running supplier balance (adding invoices, deducting payments), dispatch order requests via SMS/WhatsApp, and track payment schedules (e.g., "Chicken paid on Monday"). The owner must have a clear dashboard of "Upcoming Debt".
3. **Logistics (Lead-Time)**: 2-3 day reorder alerts to manage out-of-town suppliers effectively.
4. **Cycle Counting**: Smarter, focused counting. No tedious daily counting of all items. The system will prompt quick spot-checks for high-value items or items that had movement that day.
5. **Loss Prevention (Usage Variance)**: Comparing what was *issued* from the store vs what was *sold* in the POS. We will give the staff a simple user interface to build approximate Yield Configs (e.g., "1 Whole Chicken yields 8 Portions"). The UI must be easy for non-technical staff to understand.

## 3. CULTURAL CONTEXT
- **Informal & Busy Environment**: Requires simple, scalable, and easy-to-use interfaces. Complex systems will be ignored by staff.
- **Muslim-owned**: Barista/Kitchen focus; strictly zero alcohol/bar items.
- **Local Market**: Prioritize simple workflows over strict international GRN/Invoicing standards.
